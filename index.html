    How the chatbot works

    Five steps, no guesswork. Every step leaves a record your team can check.

    1. Answer instantlyOrder tracking, shipping, returns, stock and payments — answered from your knowledge base in under a second.
    2. Know when to stopIf the question is outside the knowledge base, the bot says so honestly instead of guessing.
    3. Escalate cleanlyIt opens a ticket, summarises the conversation, and tells the customer exactly when a human will reply.
    4. Keep the inbox tidyEvery chat is logged with topic, status and first-response time, so nothing falls through the cracks.
    5. Learn from the dataWeekly report: top questions, escalations, and the answers worth adding to the knowledge base.
    6. Stay on brandTone, greeting and escalation rules are set by you — the bot speaks like your team, not like a template.
